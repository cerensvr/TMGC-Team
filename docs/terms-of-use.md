# SkinShelf Terms of Use

Last updated: 2026-07-30

SkinShelf is a mobile application designed to help users manage their skincare products, routines, and skin tracking records.

SkinShelf uses artificial intelligence to provide personalized skincare recommendations, analyze skin photos, and assist users through the Shelly AI Assistant. AI-generated responses are provided for informational purposes only and may not always be complete or accurate.

SkinShelf is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any medical condition. Users experiencing severe skin reactions, persistent symptoms, infections, or other medical concerns should consult a qualified healthcare professional or dermatologist.

Users are responsible for the products they choose to use and should always read product labels, perform patch tests when appropriate, and discontinue use if unexpected irritation occurs.

By using SkinShelf, users acknowledge that AI-generated analyses and recommendations are intended to support skincare decisions but should not replace professional medical advice.
